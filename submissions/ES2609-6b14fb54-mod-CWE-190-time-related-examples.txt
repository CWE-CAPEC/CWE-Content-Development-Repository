NAME: Modify CWE-190 to include time-related content

DESCRIPTION:

CWE-190 currently has no time-related content. All Observed Examples are
memory-corruption chains, and the entry text does not mention timestamp
arithmetic. Integer overflow of time values is a distinct manifestation of
this weakness. It has a fixed deadline (signed 32-bit time_t wraps at
03:14:07 UTC on 19 January 2038), it already has published CVEs mapped to
CWE-190 by CISA/ICS-CERT, and its consequences (authentication failure,
denial of service, log corruption, loss of safety functions) differ from
the buffer-overflow chains the entry currently illustrates. Adding one
observed example and two short demonstrative examples would make the entry
representative of this class and help raise awareness at the same time.
This class is subject of active work under the ITU-T SG17 Technical Paper
XSTP.epoch and IEEE P4150.

I have observed examples and code to share, but this does not fit all in
5000 chars.
